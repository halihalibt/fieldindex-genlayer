OBSERVATION PROTOCOL

From forecast
to evidence.

Seal

Enter eight probabilities for Kp reaching 5 in each three-hour UTC window. Your wallet seals a salted hash before the round opens for reveals.

Reveal

Reveal the same probabilities with the original salt. A live round closes commits at the start of its UTC day and accepts reveals through the following day.

Settle

GenLayer validators independently fetch the same eight definitive GFZ values. The contract scores all matching reveals by mean Brier loss, where lower is better.

Definitive values are published monthly. Replays use archived observations for practice and stay separate from live forecasting. GFZ Helmholtz Centre for Geosciences · CC BY 4.0.
